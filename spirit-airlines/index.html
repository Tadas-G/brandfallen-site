The Night They Repossessed Spirit Airlines

In 2015, Spirit Airlines earned $317 million. On May 2, 2026, it stopped flying at 3 a.m. while repo crews were already moving for its jets. This is how America's most complained-about airline got rich, and what happened when everyone copied it.
