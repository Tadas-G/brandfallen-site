Privacy Policy

Who we are

Brandfallen is a YouTube channel and this website, brandfallen.com. The Brandfallen application is an internal tool used by the channel's owner to produce and manage the channel's own videos.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and has no accounts or forms. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

The Brandfallen application and Google data

The application signs in only to the Brandfallen channel's own Google account, and it is not offered to the public. With that account's permission it uses YouTube API Services and Google Sheets to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails, descriptions and captions to the Brandfallen channel;
  • read the channel's own video and analytics data;
  • read public information about other YouTube videos and channels for topic research;
  • keep production records in the channel's own spreadsheet.

Sign-in tokens and this data are stored on the owner's computer and in the channel's own Google account. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. Selected public information may be sent to service providers that process it for us, such as Anthropic for research and writing assistance.

YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with the application you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account's security settings. Stored data can be deleted on request.

Children

Our content is made for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
