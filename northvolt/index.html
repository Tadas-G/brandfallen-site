$13 Billion, One Week Left: How Northvolt Collapsed

November 2024: Northvolt, the company meant to be Europe's answer to China in electric-car batteries, told a US court it had 30 million dollars left, about one week of cash, against 5.8 billion in debt. It had raised about 13 billion. This is the story of Peter Carlsson, the former Tesla executive who built it, and the one thing his company never managed to do.
