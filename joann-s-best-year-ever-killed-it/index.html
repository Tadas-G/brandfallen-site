Joann's Best Year Ever Killed It

In 2020, Joann added more than 8 million customers and made about $212 million. Five years later, all 800 stores were gone and 19,000 people were out of work. This is the story of a Cleveland shop that became America's biggest craft brand, took on debt, and went bankrupt twice in under a year.
