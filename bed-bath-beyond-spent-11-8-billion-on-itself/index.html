Bed Bath & Beyond Spent $11.8 Billion on Itself

When Bed Bath & Beyond went bankrupt it owed about $5.2 billion. Over the years before that, it had spent about $11.8 billion buying back its own stock. This is the story of a company with no long-term debt that borrowed to buy its own shares, and of a name later sold for $21.5 million.
