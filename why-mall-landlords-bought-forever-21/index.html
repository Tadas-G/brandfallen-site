Why Mall Landlords Bought Forever 21

In 2020, a shopping mall company helped buy one of its own tenants out of bankruptcy for $81 million. Forever 21 had once sold more than $4 billion of clothes in a year. Then its biggest rival became its partner, and it still went bankrupt again.
