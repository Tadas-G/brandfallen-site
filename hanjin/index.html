The Ships No Port Would Let In: Hanjin Shipping

September 2016. More than 90 ships, by one count, were stuck at sea with around 14 billion dollars of cargo, and no port would let them in. This is the story of Hanjin Shipping, told backwards: from the stranded ships to the decisions that sank South Korea's biggest container line years before anyone noticed.
