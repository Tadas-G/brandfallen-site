The $267 Million Store That Killed Barneys

In January 2019, one rent bill on Madison Avenue nearly doubled. Thirteen months later, Barneys New York was gone. This is the story of how a shop opened with a pawned ring became America's coolest store, and why the family that built it never owned the palace it built.
